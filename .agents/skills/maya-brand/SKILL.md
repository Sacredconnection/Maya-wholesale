# Maya Brand

## Start every brand task

1. Read [references/brand-guidelines.md](references/brand-guidelines.md) before drafting or designing.
2. Identify the deliverable, audience, language, channel, and decision it supports.
3. Decide which archetype leads:
   - Let **Sábio** lead when trust, purchase, use, provenance, compliance, support, or technical clarity is at stake.
   - Let **Mago** lead when origin, tradition, ceremony, transformation, or brand meaning is at stake.
4. Keep both present. Let one lead and the other temper; never make a piece purely clinical or purely mystical.
5. Apply the final coherence test: sustain trust and honor the sacred at the same time.

## Protect the strategic core

- Present Maya as the **trusted bridge between ancestral cultures and people seeking reconnection today**.
- Treat trust as something proven through evidence, traceability, quality, transparency, and respect—not as self-praise.
- Preserve the balance between the material world (product, operation, precision, regulation) and the symbolic world (origin, ancestry, ceremony, sacredness).
- Credit the people, tribe, community, region, or country that holds the knowledge whenever the information is available.
- Describe Maya as a mediator and steward of access; do not position it as the owner of the plant, tradition, or ancestral knowledge.

## Write in the Maya voice

- Keep serenity constant: calm, assured, spacious, and free of pressure.
- For **Sábio-led** copy, be clear, verifiable, organized, useful, and precise. Inform before enchanting.
- For **Mago-led** copy, be deep, symbolic, rooted, and rhythmic without becoming vague, theatrical, or ornamental.
- State traditional uses naturally and anchor claims in tradition: prefer “traditionally used for” or equivalent phrasing.
- Do not make clinical promises, guarantee outcomes, imitate pharmaceutical language, or overstate evidence.
- Do not use fabricated scarcity, countdowns, fear, or aggressive urgency. Convert through confidence.
- Preserve the requested language and locale while keeping proper names, provenance, and cultural terms accurate.

## Apply the visual system

- Use terracotta `#CC6633` and olive `#999933` as the primary recognition colors.
- Use supporting families only as documented in the reference; select luminosity variants for hierarchy and contrast rather than inventing new hues.
- Use Futura PT only for the institutional nucleus; use Proxima Nova for print/production content and Roboto for web content.
- Reuse an approved logo asset. Do not redraw, stretch, crop, recolor casually, alter the symbol/wordmark proportion, or violate its protection area.
- Preserve the distinction between institutional identity, legal entity, and product-line lockups.
- Treat patterns as supporting texture. Keep them subordinate to content and logo, and reuse the existing vector asset when working in this repository.

## Choose the register by deliverable

| Deliverable | Lead | Required qualities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product page, catalog, FAQ, instructions | Sábio | Provenance, traditional use, composition, limits, clear next step |
| Wholesale/B2B, legal, policy, support | Sábio | Reliability, documentation, predictability, direct language |
| Origin story, community profile, editorial | Mago | Respectful context, named guardians, rooted narrative, factual base |
| Ceremony or tradition content | Mago | Reverence, depth, restraint, cultural specificity |
| Campaign | Choose by objective | One clear lead; preserve both trust and soul |
| UI and digital product | Sábio | Roboto, accessible contrast, calm hierarchy; let imagery and restrained pattern carry the Mago layer |

## Handle uncertainty

- Treat logo construction, protection area, color system, type hierarchy, and cultural representation as rules.
- Treat photography, contextual tone balance, and pattern application as recommendations guided by intent.
- Prefer the August 2026 brand book over conflicting legacy styling. Flag conflicts instead of silently blending systems.
- Do not invent missing lockups, font weights, cultural facts, provenance, medical evidence, or color values.
- Consult `PREVIA-BRAND-BOOK-MAYA.pdf` at the repository root when exact visual geometry or an untranscribed example is decisive.

## Review before delivery

Confirm that the result:

- makes Maya recognizable without relying on the logo alone;
- proves rather than merely declares trust;
- names origin and knowledge holders where possible;
- has a clearly leading Sábio or Mago register without losing the other;
- remains serene and avoids urgency or clinical guarantees;
- uses only documented colors, typography roles, and approved logo assets;
- respects accessibility, legibility, and the operational needs of the channel;
- honors both the material and symbolic sides of the brand.
