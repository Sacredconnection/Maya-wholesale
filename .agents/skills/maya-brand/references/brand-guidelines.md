## Governance

### Central principle

Hold two worlds in balance:

- **Material:** product, operation, precision, delivery, evidence, regulation.
- **Symbolic:** Amazonian origin, ancestral knowledge, ceremony, sacredness.

Reject applications that become only commercial, because they lose the soul. Reject applications that become only mystical, because they lose trust. Approve what sustains trust and honors the sacred simultaneously.

### Rules versus recommendations

Treat as non-negotiable rules:

- logo construction and proportions;
- logo protection area;
- color system;
- typographic hierarchy;
- principles of cultural representation.

Treat as recommendations governed by intent:

- photographic direction;
- balance of tone by context;
- application of graphic patterns.

When the document does not anticipate a situation, protect brand coherence and return to the central principle.

## Brand foundation

### Origin and authority

- Recognize Maya as founded in 2000, among the early global platforms to offer ethnobotanical plants and shamanic tools.
- Ground authority in more than two decades of continuity and durable relationships—not in marketing rhetoric.
- Treat relationships with communities, farmers, artisans, and collectors across continents as the core asset; treat the catalog as a consequence.

### The 2022 union

- Recognize the merger with Sacred Connection, a Brazilian group dedicated to Rapé and other traditional Indigenous medicines, cultural preservation, community-led cultivation, training, and spiritual preservation.
- Recognize the Dutch partners' contribution in botanical research and development, bio-extraction, product innovation, distribution, and European relationships.
- Present Maya as an alliance between ancestral knowledge/direct forest relationships and scientific rigor/European structure.

### Two connected homes

- **Nova Friburgo, Rio de Janeiro:** direct sourcing with tribes and communities, production, export, and local foundation initiatives.
- **Haarlem, Netherlands:** distribution, development, and relationships with European partners.

Use “bridge between worlds” as a description of the actual business structure, not as empty metaphor.

### Purpose

Sustain the crossing between knowledge holders and people seeking legitimate access from another culture or continent. Position Maya as the condition that enables the encounter—not as the plant, the guardian, or the seeker.

### What Maya defends

1. **Honor and preserve traditional plant knowledge.** Tie knowledge to the people who developed and maintain it. Name the tribe, community, or country of origin as part of the product.
2. **Support Indigenous communities and ethical sourcing.** Prefer direct relationships with tribes, farmers, artisans, and collectors; treat traceability as an operating condition.
3. **Connect ancestral wisdom and contemporary knowledge.** Use research, bio-extraction, and formulation to extend the reach of ancestral knowledge, not replace it.
4. **Offer potent, pure, purposeful medicines.** Require preserved potency, freedom from adulteration, and a clear purpose. Do not add products only because demand exists.
5. **Restore the bond between people and nature.** Provide context, origin, and use guidance; a contextless product does not fulfill the commitment.

## Positioning and promise

### Brand essence

Use this central positioning:

> Maya is the trusted bridge between ancestral cultures and people seeking reconnection today.

Use the English brand line when appropriate:

> Your trusted source for ethnobotanical herbs.

### Trust as a verifiable promise

Prove trust through four concrete deliveries:

1. **Traceable provenance:** state where the product comes from and identify the tribe, community, region, or country.
2. **Declared quality standard:** address preserved strength, absence of adulteration, and absence of contamination when verified.
3. **Transparency as method:** explain what is sold, how it is obtained, and what it traditionally does.
4. **Respect for origin at every stage:** support communities, credit knowledge, and return resources to its holders.

Never use these as unsupported claims. Supply the evidence available for the specific product or context.

### Market position

Position Maya as the trusted reference in sacred ethnobotany for a European audience seeking reconnection without sacrificing provenance.

Operate at the intersection of:

- spirituality and shamanism;
- natural health and wellbeing;
- regulated European commerce.

Differentiate through transparency, high quality standards, and ethical values that do not bend to commercial convenience.

Do not present Maya as:

- a head shop;
- a generic wellness-supplement brand;
- a neutral B2B raw-material supplier;
- an esoteric store without rigor;
- a technical catalog without culture.

## Archetypes and personality

### Dual archetype

#### Sábio

Use the Sábio to gather, organize, and make knowledge accessible. Express it through catalog curation, effect-based navigation, provenance, transparency, accuracy, and regulated information. Let it sustain trust and commercial legitimacy.

Use the Sábio for:

- product pages and catalogs;
- technical information and instructions;
- customer service and problem resolution;
- wholesale communication;
- FAQ, policies, and compliance.

Make it clear, competent, and transparent.

#### Mago

Use the Mago to express transformation and crossing. Express it through tribal Rapé, ceremony, relationships with peoples of origin, and the promise of reconnection. Let it give the brand soul and distinguish it from a product catalog.

Use the Mago for:

- origin narratives;
- traditions and ceremonies;
- community stories;
- campaigns and editorials;
- foundation work.

Make it deep, symbolic, and ancestral.

### Interaction rule

Never make a piece entirely Sábio or entirely Mago. Choose a leader by asking: “Is trust or soul primarily at stake here?”

- Let Sábio lead a product page, while Mago provides context and humanity.
- Let Mago lead an origin story, while Sábio provides factual grounding.

This balance, with explicit leadership by context, is Maya's personality signature.

### Five personality traits

| Trait | Meaning | Behavior | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reverence | Genuine respect for plants and cultures of origin | Credit origin, care for information, never trivialize the sacred | Warm, not liturgical; excess alienates newcomers |
| Authority | Real command of the offer | Explain deeply, guide newcomers, prove assertions | Generous, not arrogant; excess intimidates |
| Frankness | Truth about origin, use, and limits | State promises and refusals clearly | Careful, not dry; excess becomes harsh |
| Rootedness | Living link to ancestry in the present | Honor tradition while using contemporary science and structure | Living roots, not nostalgia; excess traps the brand in the past |
| Serenity | Calm, secure presence | Give people space to decide; project confidence without manufactured urgency | Present, not distant |

Keep serenity active in every register. Let authority and frankness lead commercial/institutional work; let reverence and rootedness lead editorial/ceremonial work.

## Audiences

All strategic audiences reject empty discourse and value provenance, knowledge, and respect for origin.

### Seeker

- Profile: typically 25–45, interested in meditation and neo-shamanic ceremonies, medium-high income.
- Seeks: meaningful reconnection supported by independently verifiable provenance.
- Trusts: evidence of origin and knowledge; penalizes exaggeration.
- Serve with: depth, evidence, restraint, and rich information.

### Researcher/practitioner

- Profile: therapists, ceremony facilitators, and retreat centers.
- Seeks: reliable, documented supply.
- Trusts: respect for the seriousness of the work.
- Serve as: a reputation partner whose credibility supports recommendation.

### B2B buyer

- Profile: specialist stores, retreat centers, and resellers.
- Seeks: consistency, regularity, and a supplier whose reputation strengthens their own.
- Trusts: origin, quality, and predictability.
- Serve with: a commercial relationship anchored in Maya's established reputation and operational evidence.

## Verbal identity

### Sábio register

Write clearly, verifiably, and in an organized sequence. Inform before enchanting.

Use for product, catalog, technical sheet, provenance, instructions, support, wholesale, FAQ, and policy content.

Example structure:

1. Name the product and botanical name.
2. State provenance.
3. State the traditional use.
4. Describe form, composition, preparation, or application.
5. State relevant limits, cautions, or evidence status.
6. Give a calm, unambiguous next step.

### Mago register

Write with depth, symbolism, rhythm, and weight. Give the brand soul without losing factual ground.

Use for origin, tradition, ceremony, category openings, campaigns, editorials, and foundation content.

Anchor symbolic language in specific people, places, practices, and history. Avoid vague mysticism, exoticization, and decorative spirituality.

### Effects and claims

Follow this rule: **speak from tradition; do not promise like a laboratory.**

Prefer constructions such as:

- “Traditionally used for…”
- “Valued in [tradition/community] for…”
- “Associated in traditional practice with…”
- “Traditionally prepared/applied…”

Avoid:

- guaranteed healing or cure claims;
- “clinically proven” unless substantiated and legally appropriate;
- first-person pharmaceutical certainty;
- language that erases uncertainty or individual variation;
- fabricated scarcity, countdowns, fear, or pressure.

Let Maya convert through trust, not pressure.

### Language and translation

- Preserve the user's requested locale.
- Keep botanical names, tribe and community names, and place names accurate.
- Translate meaning without flattening culturally specific concepts.
- Do not use “tribal,” “shamanic,” “sacred,” or “ancestral” as generic atmosphere; connect each term to a truthful context.
- Verify claims and provenance before translating them into stronger language.

## Visual identity

### Logo system

- Build all measurements from the modular `X` unit.
- Keep the isolated symbol at `6X` high on a single bilateral symmetry axis.
- Preserve the circular protection area shown in the source; allow no competing element inside it.
- Keep symbol and wordmark at a fixed proportion. Scale them together, never independently.
- Reduce the whole logo when space is constrained; never compress its protection area.
- Preserve the lockup structure: symbol + `MAYA` + descriptor.

Distinguish lockup levels:

- **Institutional identity:** `MAYA Ethnobotanicals`.
- **Legal entity:** `MAYA World Trading`; reserve for legal documents and contexts, not product labels.
- **Line extensions:** examples include `Incense`, `Herbs`, and `Superfoods`; use only for the matching catalog division.

Prefer the vertical version for square or vertical spaces such as avatars, seals, narrow packaging, and contexts where a horizontal signature becomes too small. Keep the vertical axis and fixed proportions intact.

Do not recreate the logo from text or approximate geometry. Request or reuse an approved vector asset. In this repository, inspect `public/banner/maya-wholesale/logo-maya-wholesale.svg` for the wholesale-specific lockup; do not substitute unrelated files by filename alone.

### Typography

Keep each family in its assigned territory.

| Family | Role | Uses | Weights |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Futura PT | Institutional | Wordmark, signature, legal data, company name, address | Light, Medium, Demi, Bold |
| Proxima Nova | Print/production system | Titles, subtitles, body, microcopy, notices, regulated information | Bold for titles, Semibold for subtitles, Regular for body, Medium for support |
| Roboto | Web system | All digital content where the licensed print families do not apply | Choose weights to preserve the same hierarchy |

Do not use Futura PT to compose general content. Do not mix Proxima Nova and Roboto on one surface merely for style. When a font is unavailable, request a licensed asset or use the declared Roboto web system; do not invent a new official family.

### Patterns

Treat patterns as a secondary graphic layer, not as primary content. Preserve their geometric, interconnected character and keep them visually subordinate to the logo and information hierarchy.

In this repository, reuse `public/patterns/maya-brand-pattern.svg` instead of tracing or approximating it. The current vector uses olive `#999933` linework at restrained opacity; preserve that restraint unless the task explicitly calls for a documented variant.

### Photography and cultural representation

The source establishes representation as a rule but gives more visual than textual guidance in the supplied preview. Apply these conservative operational safeguards:

- Prefer documentary, specific, grounded imagery of real plants, places, people, craft, and ceremony.
- Identify people and communities accurately when permission and metadata allow.
- Avoid anonymous “tribal” mood imagery, costume-like styling, exoticization, staged mysticism, and generic wellness stock photography.
- Do not use sacred objects or ceremonies as decorative props.
- Preserve dignity, consent, context, and provenance.
- When usage rights, consent, or cultural context are uncertain, pause and request confirmation.

These safeguards derive from the brand foundation. Consult the source or a brand owner for uses not explicitly documented.

## Application guidance

### Product page

Let Sábio lead. Include product/botanical name, traceable origin, traditional use, format, composition, preparation or application, verified quality information, cautions, and a calm call to action. Let Mago appear through precise origin context and respectful imagery.

### Editorial or community story

Let Mago lead. Name the people and place, explain the tradition in its own context, and build rhythm without vagueness. Let Sábio provide dates, names, geography, sourcing facts, and boundaries.

### Wholesale/B2B

Let Sábio lead strongly. Emphasize documented supply, consistency, regularity, traceability, quality, commercial predictability, and reputation. Keep cultural origin visible so Maya never sounds like a neutral commodity supplier.

### Interface

Use Roboto, clear hierarchy, accessible color contrast, restrained motion, and calm calls to action. Use primary terracotta and olive for recognition; use darker documented variants for contrast. Let photography and subtle pattern carry the symbolic layer without reducing usability.

### Campaign

Choose one strategic objective and one leading archetype. Avoid unsupported transformation promises. Let symbolic language rest on real origin, relationships, or practices, and let every commercial action retain evidence and transparency.

## Approval checklist

- Does the work sustain trust and honor the sacred simultaneously?
- Is the leading archetype clear, with the other still present?
- Is serenity audible or visible throughout?
- Are provenance, people, and knowledge holders credited accurately?
- Are all product and effect claims appropriately anchored and supported?
- Is the work free from pressure, fake scarcity, fear, and clinical guarantees?
- Does the logo use an approved asset with fixed proportions and protection area?
- Are all colors drawn from the documented system?
- Does typography respect institutional, print, and web roles?
- Are pattern and photography supporting meaning rather than decorating spirituality?
- Is the result legible, accessible, and fit for its channel?
- Does it avoid making Maya look like a head shop, generic supplement brand, esoteric store, or neutral raw-material supplier?
